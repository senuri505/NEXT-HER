✨ Who We Are

Catalyzing Potential, Building Leaders

The NEXT HER initiative by the Leo Club of USJ addresses real-world challenges faced by young women in Sri Lanka by bridging tech education with practical leadership.

"Empowering a woman in technology empowers an entire nation."

Our digital platform brings together school students, university undergraduates, mentors, and corporate partners to foster innovation, software creation, and career growth.

Through hands-on app development challenges, digital safety sessions, and leadership forums, we inspire young women to take bold steps into the digital future.

🗺️ Project Journey

Professional Project Timeline

A structured 6-phase roadmap guiding participants from registration and school workshops to the App Hackathon & Grand Finale.

OCTOBER 2026 Phase 01

Campaign Launch & Press Release

Official press conference launch announcing NEXT HER across national media, university networks, and corporate partners.

  • ✓ Official Media & Press Release
  • ✓ Website & Delegate Portal Online Launch
  • ✓ University Advisory Board Onboarding
NOVEMBER 2026 Phase 02

Delegate & Mentor Registration

Opening national registration for school girls, university hackathon teams, industry mentors, and partner organizations.

  • ✓ Registration Call for School & Uni Categories
  • ✓ App Competition Team Formation
  • ✓ Industry Mentor Matchmaking
DECEMBER 2026 Phase 03

School Awareness Drives

Conducting interactive digital safety, cyber law, and introductory coding sessions across partner schools in Sri Lanka.

  • ✓ Cyber Safety & Digital Literacy Workshops
  • ✓ STEM Career Guidance Sessions
  • ✓ Interactive School Leadership Camps
JANUARY 2027 Phase 04

App Development Hackathon Launch

Release of real-world problem statements for the App Competition. Teams commence prototyping software solutions.

  • ✓ Hackathon Problem Statement Announcement
  • ✓ Technical Architecture Briefings
  • ✓ Weekly Code Review & Check-ins
FEBRUARY 2027 Phase 05

Mentorship & Bootcamps

Intensive one-on-one sessions with senior software engineers, UI/UX experts, and tech startup founders.

  • ✓ UI/UX & Cloud Architecture Masterclasses
  • ✓ Pitch Deck & Product Demo Mentoring
  • ✓ Semi-Final Prototype Evaluation
MARCH 2027 Phase 06

Grand Finale & Awards Ceremony

Final live product demonstrations, judging panel evaluation, prize distribution, and media press conference.

  • ✓ Live App Pitching to Corporate Judges
  • ✓ Winner Awards & Incubator Grants
  • ✓ Closing Press Release & Impact Report

❓ Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NEXT HER?

▼

NEXT HER is a flagship Women Empowerment in the Digital World initiative conducted by the Leo Club of University of Sri Jayewardenepura, Lions International, Leo District 306 D2. It includes an App Development Competition, Awareness Programs, and Press Conference.

Who can enter the App Development Competition?

▼

School students and university undergraduates are invited to submit software applications. Mentors will assist team registrants during coding bootcamps.
