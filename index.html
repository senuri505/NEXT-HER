Empowering Through Digital Safety & Innovation

NEXT HER is a youth-led initiative by the Leo Club of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, focused on empowering women and girls to use technology safely, confidently, and responsibly.

“Don’t Fear Technology. Know How to Use It Safely.”

NEXT HER brings together digital safety awareness and technological innovation to address challenges affecting women and girls.

Through the Women Safe App Challenge, university students are encouraged to transform their ideas into technology-based solutions for real-world safety challenges.

The journey moves from idea submission and evaluation to the Top 10 Buildathon, creating an opportunity for young innovators to develop practical solutions and present them at the NEXT HER Digital Safety Conference.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NEXT HER?

▼

NEXT HER is a youth-led Women Empowerment in the Digital World initiative conducted by the Leo Club of University of Sri Jayewardenepura (Lions International, Leo District 306 D2). Guided by the core motto “Don’t fear technology. Know how to use it safely”, it includes School Digital Safety Seminars across 5 districts, a Women Safe App Challenge for university students, and a Digital Safety Conference.

Who can enter the App Development Competition?

▼

University undergraduates across Sri Lanka are invited to submit app proposals addressing women's safety challenges. Top 10 selected teams will proceed to the Buildathon and launch their functional apps at the NEXT HER Digital Safety Conference.

What happens during the Ideathon Submission stage?

▼

University students submit their app ideas addressing real-world challenges related to women's safety. Submitted proposals are then reviewed, and the Top 10 teams are selected to move forward to the Buildathon.

What is the Buildathon?

▼

It's where the Top 10 selected teams develop their app ideas into fully functional applications, ready to be presented at the NEXT HER Digital Safety Conference.

What is the NEXT HER Digital Safety Conference?

▼

The closing event of the project, where the finished apps are launched and professionals, advocates, and young people come together to discuss cybercrime, women's digital safety, and legal protection in Sri Lanka.
