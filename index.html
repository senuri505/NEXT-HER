✨ Who We Are

Empowering Through Digital Safety & Innovation

NEXT HER is a youth-led initiative by the Leo Club of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, focused on empowering women and girls to use technology safely, confidently, and responsibly.

“Don’t Fear Technology. Know How to Use It Safely.”

NEXT HER brings together digital safety awareness and technological innovation to address challenges affecting women and girls.

Through the Women Safe App Challenge, university students are encouraged to transform their ideas into technology-based solutions for real-world safety challenges.

The journey moves from idea submission and evaluation to the Top 10 Buildathon, creating an opportunity for young innovators to develop practical solutions and present them at the NEXT HER Digital Safety Conference.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NEXT HER?

▼

NEXT HER is a flagship Women Empowerment in the Digital World initiative conducted by the Leo Club of University of Sri Jayewardenepura, Lions International, Leo District 306 D2. It includes an App Development Competition, Awareness Programs, and Press Conference.

Who can enter the App Development Competition?

▼

School students and university undergraduates are invited to submit software applications. Mentors will assist team registrants during coding bootcamps.
